How Much Does a Bhutan Trip Cost in 2026–27?

Updated 2026-11-03 · Written with local guides at Orrog, a Bhutan-based tour operator

Bhutan is not a budget destination, but it is often less expensive than people assume. The biggest single item is the government's Sustainable Development Fee (SDF); everything else — hotels, a guide, a car and driver, meals — is usually bundled into a tour price. This page breaks down each part so you can sanity-check any quote you receive.

The Sustainable Development Fee (SDF)

The SDF is a per-person, per-night levy paid to the Royal Government of Bhutan. It funds healthcare, education and conservation, and it is the reason Bhutan can keep tourist numbers low. It was set at USD 200 when the country reopened in 2022, then halved in September 2023.

Visa, GST and other fees

  • Visa: USD 40 per person, per trip, for international visitors. Indian nationals don't need a visa but need an entry permit, issued free on arrival with a valid passport or voter ID.
  • GST: a 5% goods and services tax applies to tour components such as hotels, transport and guiding. The SDF itself is not taxed.
  • Flights: only Drukair and Bhutan Airlines fly into Paro. Fares from Delhi, Kolkata, Bagdogra, Kathmandu and Bangkok vary a lot by season, so book early for spring and autumn.
  • Monument entry fees: major dzongs and temples charge a small entry fee, usually included in tour packages.

Ways to keep costs down

  • Travel in a small group. Guide and vehicle costs are shared, so per-person prices fall noticeably for three or four travellers.
  • Go in the shoulder or low season. Winter and summer bring lower hotel rates; some hotels and regions have run SDF promotions in these months.
  • Choose 3-star or homestay options. The SDF is fixed, but accommodation is where prices vary most.
  • Book directly with a local operator. A Bhutan-based company like Orrog avoids an extra overseas agency margin.
  • Plan around one region. Bhutan's mountain roads are slow; staying in the west (Paro, Thimphu, Punakha) cuts driving days.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Bhutan SDF in 2026?

International adults pay USD 100 per person per night, a concession valid until 31 August 2027. Children aged 6–11 pay USD 50, and children under 6 are exempt.

How much is the Bhutan SDF for Indians?

Indian nationals pay ₹1,200 (Nu 1,200) per person per night. Children aged 6–11 pay ₹600 and children under 6 pay nothing.

How much is the Bhutan visa fee?

International visitors pay a one-time visa fee of USD 40 per person. Indian nationals don't need a visa but must obtain a free entry permit.

Is the SDF included in a Bhutan tour package?

Usually yes, but check your quote. Many operators list the SDF separately because it is a fixed government fee.

How much does a 7-day trip to Bhutan cost?

For an international traveller on a standard private tour, expect roughly USD 1,750–2,450 per person for seven nights, including the SDF, hotels, meals, guide and transport, but excluding international flights and the visa.
